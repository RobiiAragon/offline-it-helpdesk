# New Laptop Setup and Replacement

## Standard hardware

| Role | Model | Refresh cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Office staff | 14" Windows laptop, 16 GB RAM | 4 years |
| Engineering / data | 16" workstation laptop, 32 GB RAM | 3 years |
| Design | MacBook Pro | 4 years |

## First sign-in (Windows Autopilot)

1. Turn on the laptop and connect to Wi-Fi (`CORP-BYOD` or home Wi-Fi works; `GUEST` does not).
2. Sign in with your work email and approve MFA.
3. Autopilot installs company apps and policies. **This takes 30-60 minutes**; keep the laptop plugged in.
4. When the desktop appears, open Software Center to install optional apps.

## Replacing a broken or slow laptop

- Hardware failures under warranty are replaced; open a ticket with the asset tag on the bottom of the laptop.
- Laptops past the refresh cycle can be replaced on request.
- Before returning an old laptop, make sure OneDrive shows "Up to date". Files saved only on the desktop or in `C:\Temp` are not backed up.
- Old laptops are wiped and recycled by IT. Do not give them to colleagues.

## Admin rights

Standard users do not have local administrator rights. Use Software Center for approved
software. Temporary admin access (up to 4 hours) can be requested in the IT portal and
requires manager approval and a business justification.
