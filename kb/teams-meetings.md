# Microsoft Teams and Meeting Rooms

## Teams audio and video problems

1. In Teams, go to Settings → **Devices** and select the correct microphone, speaker and camera.
2. Make a test call: Settings → Devices → **Make a test call**.
3. Close other apps that use the camera (Zoom, browser tabs).
4. Clear the Teams cache: quit Teams, delete the folder `%appdata%\Microsoft\Teams` (classic) or reset the app in Settings → Apps → Microsoft Teams → **Reset** (new Teams), then restart.
5. On macOS, allow camera and microphone access in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

## Meeting rooms

Rooms are booked in Outlook by adding the room as an attendee. Rooms with a Teams Room
console start the meeting with one tap on the touch panel.

- Display shows "No signal": select the correct HDMI input on the TV remote, or use the console's **Share** button.
- Room console frozen: restart it from the power switch under the table; it takes about 3 minutes.
- Report room equipment faults with the room name (e.g. `B2-Monterrey`) through the IT portal.

## External guests

External guests can join Teams meetings from a browser without an account. Guest access
to Teams channels requires a request from the team owner.
