# Reporting Phishing and Security Incidents

## Recognizing phishing

Be suspicious of messages that:
- create urgency ("your account will be closed today"),
- ask for passwords, MFA codes, gift cards or payment changes,
- come from a lookalike domain such as `examp1e.com`,
- carry the yellow **[EXTERNAL]** banner but claim to be from an internal department.

## How to report

1. In Outlook, click **Report Phishing** on the ribbon. This sends the message to the Security team and removes it from your mailbox.
2. Do not forward the email to colleagues and do not click links "to check".
3. On mobile, forward the message as an attachment to `phishing@example.com`.

## If you clicked a link or entered your password

Act immediately; speed matters more than embarrassment.
1. Change your password at `https://sspr.example.com`.
2. Call the Service Desk at extension 4357 and say "security incident". This is handled 24/7.
3. If you opened an attachment, disconnect the laptop from the network (turn off Wi-Fi or unplug the cable) but **do not shut it down**, so the Security team can investigate.

## Other incidents

Lost or stolen laptops and phones, suspicious USB devices found in the building, or
someone tailgating through a badge door should also be reported at extension 4357 or to
`security@example.com`.
