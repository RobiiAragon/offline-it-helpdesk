## Priorities and response targets

| Priority | Example | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 - Critical | Production line system down, security incident | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 - High | A whole team cannot work, VPN down | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P3 - Normal | One user affected, workaround exists | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| P4 - Request | New software, access request | 1 business day | 5 business days |

## Writing a good ticket

Include what you were doing, the exact error message (a screenshot helps), when it started,
your asset tag and whether colleagues are affected. Tickets with this information are
resolved much faster.
