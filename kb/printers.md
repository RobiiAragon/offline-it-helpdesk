# Printing

All office printers use secure "follow-me" printing through PaperCut.

## Adding the printer

- **Windows:** the `FollowMe-Print` queue is installed automatically. If it is missing, open Software Center and install **PaperCut Print Deploy**.
- **macOS:** install **PaperCut Print Deploy** from Self Service, then choose `FollowMe-Print` in the print dialog.

## Releasing a job

1. Print to `FollowMe-Print` from any application.
2. Walk to any multifunction printer and tap your badge on the reader.
3. Select the job and press **Print**. Jobs not released within **24 hours** are deleted.

## Common problems

- **Badge not recognized:** first-time users must register the badge by entering their username and password on the printer screen once.
- **Job does not appear:** check that you printed to `FollowMe-Print`, not a direct IP printer. Restart the PaperCut client.
- **Paper jam or toner:** follow the instructions on the printer screen. Toner and paper are in the supply cabinet next to each printer; report hardware faults with the printer asset tag (white label, e.g. `PRN-B2-014`).
- **Color printing denied:** color is enabled per department. Your manager can request it through the IT portal.

Large-format plotters in Engineering are managed by the Engineering IT team, not the Service Desk.
