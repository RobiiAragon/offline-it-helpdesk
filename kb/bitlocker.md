# BitLocker and FileVault Recovery

All company laptops are encrypted: BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on macOS.

## Why a recovery key is requested

A BitLocker recovery screen appears after firmware (BIOS) updates, hardware changes such as
a new motherboard or docking-station firmware, or repeated wrong PIN entries.

## Getting your recovery key

1. On another device (phone or a colleague's computer), go to `https://myaccount.example.com/devices`.
2. Sign in with your work email and MFA.
3. Select your laptop and choose **View BitLocker keys**. Match the **Key ID** shown on the blue recovery screen (first 8 characters).
4. Type the 48-digit recovery key.

If you cannot sign in, call the Service Desk at extension 4357. After identity
verification, an agent reads the key to you. Recovery keys are single-use and rotate
automatically after being shown.

## macOS FileVault

If a Mac asks for a FileVault recovery key, contact the Service Desk with the serial number
(printed on the bottom of the Mac). The key is escrowed in the device-management system.

**Never** write recovery keys on a sticky note attached to the laptop.
