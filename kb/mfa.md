# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Applies to: email, VPN, the HR portal and every cloud application behind single sign-on.

## Enrolling

1. Install Microsoft Authenticator or any TOTP app on your phone.
2. Sign in at `https://mysignins.example.com` and choose **Security info → Add method**.
3. Scan the QR code, then approve the test notification.
4. Add a second method (SMS or a hardware key) as a backup.

Hardware security keys (FIDO2) are available for staff who cannot use a personal phone.
Request one in the IT portal under **Hardware → Security key**; they are issued at the
Service Desk counter in Building B.

## New phone or lost phone

- **New phone, old phone still works:** add the new phone as a method first, then remove the old one.
- **Lost or broken phone:** call the Service Desk at extension 4357. For your security, an
  agent verifies your identity with your employee ID and a video call or an in-person visit
  with your badge. MFA is then reset and you must re-enroll within 24 hours.
- Report a lost phone that had company email to the Security team immediately so the
  device can be wiped remotely.

## Unexpected MFA prompts

If you receive an MFA prompt you did not start, **deny it** and change your password at once.
This usually means someone knows your password. Report it with the "Report Phishing"
button or call extension 4357.
